FAQ

Which video formats are supported?

Standard support includes mp4, mov, m4v, avi and mkv. avi/mkv support is available on Apple Silicon.

Is data sent to external servers?

No. Processing stays on your device, with no external uploads and no tracking.

What metadata is displayed?

File name, path, size, modified date, duration and resolution.

How are videos played?

Clicking a row opens the built-in player. You can switch to your default macOS player in Settings.

Do favorites and tags work on another Mac?

Yes. Favorites and custom tags are saved in a sidecar file inside the managed folder, so they follow an external drive to another Mac.

Are folder changes picked up automatically?

No. Click Refresh in the toolbar to rescan only what was added, changed or removed.
